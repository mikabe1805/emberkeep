## Included release scope

- The owner-approved Day Ledger icon and its verified iOS artwork pipeline.
- General Daybook events and tasks, with School remaining optional.
- Month, Week, 3 Days, and Day views combining plans, classes, deadlines, and
  chosen quests.
- Stable calendar interaction: day selection preserves the visible 3-day
  spread, view state restores safely, rapid saves stay ordered, mode changes
  return to the top, and selected rows retain predictable accessible controls.
- Then & Now appears only every third local calendar day, excludes current-day
  writing, and rotates deterministically through eligible older journal entries.
- Manual locations and handoff to Apple Maps or Google Maps without requesting
  device location.
- Room Guide and gentler streak-freeze behavior already present on the release
  line.
- A six-image App Store screenshot sequence with a real Plans/Daybook frame.
- Versioned App Store copy, privacy answers, review notes, and release checks.

Android publication is deliberately deferred. `release-candidate.json`, the
Google Play screenshots, Android configuration, and Android release evidence
were not changed.

with the final source commit, binary hash, bundle identity, version/build,
signing identity, profile, SDK, and associated-domain checks. That receipt is
the authority for the signed artifact. Upload, TestFlight processing, App
Review approval, and public catalog availability are distinct states.
