# Room of Days 1.0.4+24 — local release verification

Prepared August 19, 2026 from the polished working tree based on
`dce4a4dbe2ff8b5eebf6710d77de469318ab3c68` on
`release/room-of-days-1.0.4`. The signed Codemagic receipt must identify the
later source commit that adds this verification record.

Build 23 is processed in TestFlight and remains the internal fallback. Build 24
is the only current candidate for the App Store Version 1.0.4 draft.

## Included release scope

- Everything recorded for the locally verified Build 23 candidate: the general
  Daybook, optional School lane, four calendar views, stable day interaction,
  Then & Now journal rotation, manual locations and map handoff, Room Guide,
  gentler streak freezes, and the six-image App Store story.
- A restrained five-material sound palette. Wood, stone, parchment, glass, and
  brass interactions each cycle through three short authored variants without
  runtime random pitch shifting.
- A real one-shot magic-fire ignition followed by a quiet fire tail. The Quest
  hearth begins unlit, wakes only when the room is actually visible, and stays
  lit without looping audio or replaying on tab changes and resume.
- Startup overlays and incoming room links defer the ignition without consuming
  its per-process gate. Reduce Motion lands directly on the settled flame.
- Pointer, keyboard, and assistive activations each receive exactly one matching
  material acknowledgment; scrolling and cancelled gestures cannot leak a
  stale acknowledgment into the next action.
- Featured Quests with no keeper note or curated instruction receive one calm,
  state-aware companion line. The selection rotates deterministically across
  three civil days, while factual due and overdue labels remain stable.
- The approved Day Ledger silhouette and layout remain intact; only the earned
  top-row medallion is brighter, with a tightly contained honey reflection that
  survives App Library scale.
- Versioned App Store and TestFlight copy plus a physical-device audition script
  covering sound fatigue, silent mode, music coexistence, Reduce Motion, deep
  links, and the smallest icon presentation.

Looping ambience remains deliberately out of Build 24. It needs a separate
opt-in product decision, licensed source, and long-session physical audition.
Android publication remains deferred; the shared icon derivatives are kept
coherent, but no Android candidate or release evidence was replaced.

## Local gates passed

- `flutter analyze`: no issues.
- `flutter test`: all 717 tests passed in 1 minute 15 seconds.
- The full suite covers release identity, the deterministic sound router,
  ignition ownership and animation, deep-link retries, pointer and non-pointer
  activation, Quest copy precedence and rotation, narrow layouts, icon hashes
  and exporters, visual captures, store artwork, privacy, and persistence.
- `dart run tool/verify_store_submission.dart --ios-only`: store field limits,
  1.0.4+24 identity, release notes, public URL declarations, privacy/export
  metadata, licenses, App Store icon, and six-image screenshot set passed.
- `flutter build web --release`: passed, including Flutter's Wasm dry run. This
  was a source compilation check; no web deployment was performed.
- `git diff --check`: clean apart from Git's informational CRLF notices.
- Independent visual review found no blocker in the Quest hierarchy, off-to-lit
  fire sequence, platform icon masks, or 32-pixel icon review. The result reads
  as a quiet back room after the tavern has emptied, without fantasy-roleplay
  copy or decorative cosplay.
- Independent diff review found two pre-release edge cases—deep-link ignition
  retry and non-pointer Pressable feedback. Both were fixed and covered before
  the final full-suite pass.

## Gates that still require Codemagic, Apple, or the physical iPhone

- Confirm Build 24 is unused through the live App Store Connect high-water
  check, then produce and verify the signed IPA on the pinned Codemagic macOS
  toolchain and observe successful TestFlight processing.
- Install Build 24 over processed Build 23 and also as a fresh install. Complete
  `DEVICE-ACCEPTANCE-RUNBOOK.md`, with special attention to the one-time fwoosh,
  material balance over repeated taps, Ring/Silent behavior, music coexistence,
  Reduce Motion, VoiceOver, and the 32-pixel App Library icon.
- Keep the existing App Store Version 1.0.4 draft unsent until that audition is
  accepted. Then attach processed Build 24, complete remaining owner-only
  fields, submit it to App Review, and separately verify public catalog release.

The Codemagic workflow emits `release-evidence.txt` beside the IPA with the
final source commit, binary hash, bundle identity, version/build, signing
identity, profile, SDK, and associated-domain checks. That receipt is the
authority for the signed artifact. Upload, TestFlight processing, App Review,
and public catalog availability are distinct states.
