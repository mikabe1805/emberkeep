# Room of Days 1.0.4+41 — local release verification

Prepared September 1, 2026 from the isolated Build 41 music-role worktree.
This is source and rendered-state evidence for an internal TestFlight candidate;
it is not physical-device listening approval or an App Store screenshot receipt.

## Corrected contract

- Normal Room music uses the owner-selected umbrella-brush grammar: eight
  96-second jazzy performances, shuffled without an adjacent repeat and
  crossfaded at their seams.
- Focus uses the separate 112-second peaceful meditation theme.
- Entering Focus waits until the normal role is silent. Leaving waits until the
  Focus role is paused, then restores normal music only when the saved global
  preference remains enabled.
- Focus-local opt-in and one-tap quiet never rewrite the global preference.
- Both roles remain optional, pause outside the foreground, mix with other
  audio, and fail silent rather than blocking the app.
