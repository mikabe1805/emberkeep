## Gates that still require Apple, Codemagic, or a physical iPhone

- Confirm App Store Connect's current 1.0 status, public US availability,
  agreement state, and that Build 22 is unused.
- Produce and verify the signed App Store IPA on the pinned Codemagic macOS
  toolchain, then observe its successful TestFlight processing.
- Install Build 22 over Build 19 or 21 on a physical iPhone, then complete the
  focused persistence, Daybook, offline, accessibility, performance, and maps
  checks in `DEVICE-ACCEPTANCE-RUNBOOK.md`.
- Attach the accepted build to Version 1.0.4, complete owner-only legal/contact
  fields, submit it to App Review, and verify the released version in Apple's
  public catalog.

The Codemagic workflow emits a separate `release-evidence.txt` beside the IPA
with the final source commit, binary hash, bundle identity, version/build,
signing identity, profile, SDK, and associated-domain checks. That receipt is
the authority for the signed artifact.
