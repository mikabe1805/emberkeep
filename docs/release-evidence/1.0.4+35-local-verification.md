# Room of Days 1.0.4+35 — local release verification

Prepared August 29, 2026 from the isolated Build 35 candidate on
`release/room-of-days-1.0.4-build-35`. Build 34 is the last locally proven
Apple high-water mark. The Codemagic workflow must still confirm the live
App Store Connect high-water mark before signing or uploading this build.

This is an internal TestFlight candidate. It does not authorize or claim a
public App Store submission.

## Included release scope

- Goals now open into a living room and an explicit Workshop handoff. The
  steward helps shape a route, presents meaningfully different poses, and only
  offers the route card when a user-facing handoff is actually happening.
- The first Quest is not created until the person explicitly accepts the route
  in the Workshop. Leaving and returning preserves that decision boundary.
- Goal planning and recovery are domain-neutral. School, home, movement,
  creative, work, and other goals use the same planning and support value.
- A difficult-day recovery path can make the cut smaller, prepare the next
  return, or leave today alone. Leaving today alone performs no durable Goal or
  Quest mutation; revised work waits for Workshop acceptance.
- Repeatable Quests repair legacy over-cap states such as 16/5, rise beyond the
  original target, and gain a richer mastery frame as practice continues.
- The Workshop is composed from separate rear-room, steward, and foreground
  planes. Five registered steward poses preserve the same identity, outfit,
  and two sharpened wooden pencils.
- Interaction sound now belongs to the accepted outcome rather than pointer
  contact. A scroll cancellation stays quiet, ordinary accepted controls answer
  once, and Quest completion retains its longer-settle composite.
- Build 35 has a matching factual What's New record covering the Workshop,
  rising Quest mastery, and the refined material sound language.
