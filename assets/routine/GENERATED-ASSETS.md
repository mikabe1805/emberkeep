## Generation and integration direction

- Preserve authored leather grain, paper fibers, worn gilding, beveled brass,
  page stacks, stitching, hinge hardware, and perspective. Do not replace
  these with flat color blocks or procedural approximations.
- Keep app copy and quest data live. The shell, clasp, tray, ribbon, bookmark,
  and rule assets contain only physical ornament that benefits from authored
  raster detail.
- A selected nightly priority receives a real cloth ribbon emerging from the
  lower seam of its numbered slot. The same saved 1–2–3 order drives the
  morning page, where rank one becomes a quiet live `1 BEGIN HERE` letterpress
  line rather than a second floating bookmark or competing brass plate.
- Brass sheen responds gently to scroll and user tilt. The base material
  remains richly varied while parked; it does not emit autonomous sparkles.
- The completed-thread record shows a readable sample and an inline
  `and N more!` disclosure when volume exceeds the designed page rhythm.
- Chroma-key generations were converted to real alpha, checked for halos, and
  exported as high-quality WebP. Original ImageGen outputs remain in the Codex
  generation archive; these are the checked-in app derivatives.
