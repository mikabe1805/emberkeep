Install it

  1. Download the file, then open it from your browser or Downloads.
  2. If Android asks, allow that browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. You can turn that browser permission off again afterward.

Android may warn that this did not come from the Play Store. That is expected for this preview; only use the download linked from roomofdays.com.

Keep your save

Your progress stays on your phone unless you choose cloud backup. Before moving to the eventual Play Store edition, open Me → Your save is yours and export a backup.

If anything feels wrong, confusing, or unsafe for your progress, write to support@roomofdays.com.
