Room of Days support

If something feels wrong, confusing, or unsafe for your progress, tell us.

Email support@roomofdays.com.

What to include

  • what you expected and what happened instead;
  • whether you are using iPhone, Android, or the web app; and
  • the app version, if you can reach it.

Never send your password or an unredacted journal export. If your save is still available, export a backup before resetting or reinstalling.
