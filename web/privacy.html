Privacy at Room of Days

Last updated August 3, 2026

Your device is the source of truth. Room of Days works without an account, advertising, or analytics.

Data on your device

Your quests, goals, progress, journal text, preferences, and space are stored locally. Journal photos are not included in cloud backup. They stay on your device unless you separately choose one as a shared profile or This season photo.

Cloud backup and optional account

Cloud backup is off by default. If you turn it on, Room of Days creates an anonymous Firebase session and mirrors your save to Cloud Firestore. Sharing or visiting a space may also create an anonymous Firebase session, but does not turn on full-save backup. If you create an account, Firebase Authentication stores your email and links the cloud save to your sign-in so it can follow you across devices. Firebase acts as a service provider. Room of Days does not sell data or use it for ads.

Shared spaces

Sharing is optional. A shared space contains your level, an app-generated build title, preset room appearance, and a broad recent-activity state. Your visitor page is private by default. If you explicitly choose Open my visitor page, the shared space also contains your display name and only the profile cards you mark for visitors. About and Right now can contain a short introduction and up to three selected goals. Pinned moments and This season can contain bounded writing from those cards. You may also separately allow one profile photo and one This season photo. Only those selected images are uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage; changing the photo, turning its visitor access off, stopping sharing, resetting, or deleting the account removes the corresponding shared object. Shared spaces never include other journal photos or pages, quest details, streak history, email, sign-in credentials, or account-profile details. The backend room record carries an anonymous Firebase owner ID so security rules can restrict changes to its owner; the same ID forms the private owner segment of a selected photo's Storage path. Room of Days does not display that ID as profile identity. Anyone with the room link can view the selected visitor cards and selected photos until you turn them off or stop sharing. You can close the visitor page, change any card or photo audience, or stop sharing from the same controls that show your code.

If you keep someone else’s room in your Circle or send a preset support signal, Firestore stores a fixed, text-free receipt with a timestamp and your anonymous Firebase user ID. Only that room’s owner can read or clear it. No custom message, profile text, or contact information is attached.

Deletion and control

You can export or reset your data from Me → Your save is yours. A reset removes on-device data, the cloud save, any shared space, and the anonymous Firebase identity used for guest backup; Room of Days then starts a fresh blank guest profile. If the network interrupts that cleanup, the app keeps the old guest credential and opaque room code only long enough to retry, rather than leaving shared data behind with no owner able to remove it. Signed-in users keep their login after a normal reset and can permanently delete that account from Me → Your account → Delete account. See the account deletion guide.

Permissions

Photo and camera access is used only when you choose to attach media to a journal entry. Notification permission is used only when you enable local reminders. Room of Days does not track you across apps or websites.
