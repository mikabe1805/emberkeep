Privacy at Emberkeep

Last updated July 10, 2026

Your device is the source of truth. Emberkeep works without an account, advertising, or analytics.

Data on your device

Your quests, goals, progress, journal text, preferences, and keep are stored locally. Journal photos stay on your device and are not included in cloud backup or shared keeps.

Cloud backup and optional account

To protect against browser or device loss, Emberkeep creates an anonymous Firebase session and mirrors your save to Cloud Firestore. If you create an account, Firebase Authentication also stores your email and links that save to your sign-in so it can follow you across devices. Firebase acts as a service provider. Emberkeep does not sell data or use it for ads.

Shared keeps

Sharing is optional. A shared keep contains only the display name you chose, level, build title, room appearance, and whether the hearth is lit. It never includes quests, journal entries, email, or account details. You can stop sharing from the same dialog that shows your code.

Deletion and control

You can export or reset local data from Me → Your save is yours. Signed-in users can permanently delete their account, cloud backup, shared keep, and on-device Emberkeep data from Me → Your account → Delete account. See the account deletion guide.

Permissions

Photo and camera access is used only when you choose to attach media to a journal entry. Notification permission is used only when you enable local reminders. Emberkeep does not track you across apps or websites.
