# Room of Days sonic world v1

Status: **the shared everyday family passed blind desktop cohesion and semantic
distinction; Answered detent won the focused completion follow-up; the everyday
family then failed two physical-phone gates.** The focused v2 correction only
felt fun in a rapid cadence and still sounded unfinished one click at a time.
Nothing here is wired into the app.

## The correction

The prior pass answered, “Which clean tap belongs to which action?” The current
feedback shows that was one level too shallow. The sounds had different names
and envelopes, but not one acoustic origin. They read as a curated sample
folder.

Room of Days needs one imagined cause:

> A close, handmade ledger mechanism inside a warm living room responds to the
> hand. Small actions expose only its contact. Meaningful actions reveal more
> of its body, tuning, breath, and room.

This is not literal ledger Foley. It is an authored physical illusion, just as
the useful part of the Waves reference is the transformed tapping gesture—not
the stones.

## Shared acoustic physics

Every foreground cue must inherit all of these decisions:

| Dimension | Room rule |
| --- | --- |
| Contact | One soft-edged, close mechanism impulse; readable inside 10 ms; no brittle 4–8 kHz spike. |
| Body | One damped modal family concentrated in the phone-readable 550–1400 Hz range, with quiet 250–450 Hz support. |
| Notes | D-major pentatonic only. Frequent taps expose at most one quiet note; outcomes may reveal a root/fifth or a two-note answer. |
| Texture | Clean micro-grain with a rounded top; breath/ember appears only when an event opens into the room. |
| Space | Centered and intimate, with one nearly subliminal 18–35 ms warm reflection fingerprint. No audible old-studio tail. |
| Dynamics | Importance is expressed through body, duration, width, and silence—not volume alone. Rapid taps soften globally. |

The pitch field is not an emotional story. It is simply a compatibility rule so
note movement across clicks is playful instead of random.

## One mechanism, different gestures

```text
same contact/body/notes/space
        |
        +-- open       short outward tak
        +-- select     small two-part detent
        +-- navigate   weighted downward dak
        +-- place      warmer confirmed seat
        +-- complete   catch, then rounded bloom
        +-- discover   body opens into breath and room
        +-- ceremony   sparse phrase made from the same world
```

The categories are semantic. A glass-looking card and a parchment-looking page
can use the same `open` gesture because the user performed the same action.
Visual material may shade an authoring layer later, but it cannot replace the
event identity.

## Rhythm and variation

- Each frequent role gets three to five close takes with no immediate repeat.
- A single global phrase walk chooses compatible notes across controls. There
  is no independent random note generator per widget.
- A run can form a gentle played figure, but a single tap remains complete and
  never waits for the next note.
- Fast mixed tapping reduces gain and tail density. It does not build an
  accidental louder beat.

## Mix and silence

Priority is `ceremony > discovery > completion > placement > navigation >
contact > ambient`. At most two ordinary voices coexist. Completion or
discovery clears ordinary tails before it opens.

Already-selected tabs, unchanged choices, scrolls, typing, loading, passive
previews, busy/disabled controls, cancel/not-now, and background sync are
silent. Silence is part of the system, not a missing asset.

## What is approved and what is not

- Candidate C remains the approved **weighted contact gesture** for navigation.
  It is an anchor, not a requirement that every future master remain
  byte-for-byte identical.
- The current `complete.wav` remains a useful **rounded bloop meaning**. It is
  not approved as the final cohesive completion master.
- The semantic roles are directionally settled.
- The previous four-sound family failed the world/cohesion gate even though the
  files were technically clean.
- No new runtime routing should happen until a normal cross-role flow sounds
  like one Room on a physical phone.

## First proof result

The browser lab presented one A/B system test from a single shared contact,
modal body, provisional pitch field, and room fingerprint:

1. `navigate -> open -> select -> place -> complete`
2. previous mixed study as the control;
3. unified Room family as the candidate;
4. same cadence and comparable perceived level;
5. phone speaker first, headphones second.

The unified candidate won the level-controlled one-Room question and its verbs
remained distinct. The previous mixed study won the first natural hierarchy
pass. The focused follow-up therefore kept open/select/navigate/place
byte-identical and changed only the earned completion subgraph. Answered detent
won the matched tournament and then the natural comparison against the previous
reward; the owner's reason was: “I like the composed stuff”. See
`../room-reward-voice-v1/LISTENING-RESULT.md`.
