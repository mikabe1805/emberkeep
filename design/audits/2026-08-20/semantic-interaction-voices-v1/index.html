Room of Days · sound lab

Four different interaction voices.

This is no longer a material comparison. Each card represents a different thing the app is doing, so clicking around can feel varied without becoming noisy or inconsistent.

C is untouched. The page-switch Dak is the exact approved C family. Finish Quest uses the exact current completion Bloop after a small accepted-state touch.

Start once to unlock and prewarm every sound. The first comparison click will then be properly synchronized.

Click around by meaning

Judge whether the verbs feel different—not whether the words are perfect.

Dak · move

Switch page

For accepted tabs, pages, major sections, or moving the calendar to another period or view. Firm enough to make destination changes feel physical.

Tak · open

Open journal entry

For opening a card, entry, detail view, sheet, or ordinary light button. Shorter, drier, and quicker than C.

Tuk · settle

Choose a date

For a date, toggle, filter, picker, or pin visibly settling into a new state. Rounder and smaller than page movement.

Bloop · earn

Finish quest

The current rounded completion sound, reserved for a quest or routine that actually finished. It follows a small accepted-state touch.

Hear them as one app

Page → journal → choose → choose → open → finish. This is the useful test: does the sequence feel varied, coherent, and fun?

The best feedback now is something like “Tak is too sharp,” “Tuk and Dak still feel too similar,” or “yes, these finally feel like four different actions.”
