# Room of Days C Gesture v3

Focused physical-phone audition after `room-phone-click-v2`.

## Question

Which gesture makes a single ordinary click feel complete and satisfying? C keeps the complete old waveform shape and closure the user specifically remembered. X is one meaningfully more seated challenger. A common `+1.5 dB` audition lift is applied equally; level is not a study axis. The raw approved C files remain byte-identical under `anchor/` for lineage proof.

## Protocol

- iPhone built-in speaker, full volume, portrait, Bluetooth/headphones disconnected.
- The primary gate requires a natural isolated single and natural slow six-click sequence for C and X.
- Primary choices are C, X, or neither. `neither` stops the study immediately.
- Rapid playback is diagnostic only and cannot unlock the primary choice.
- Confirmation requires the selected single, full Room flow, and rapid diagnostic to finish naturally before `ready`, `almost`, or `no` can be recorded.
- Answered Detent remains locked and is not compared here.

The page exports route attestation, device/noise/orientation, common lift, exact system mappings, natural-completion evidence, interruption events, choices, and the verbatim reason. `c-role-family` remains available for engineering inspection but is deliberately omitted from the human comparison because it measured too close to C to justify another ambiguous choice.

## Result

X (`c-clasp-family`) won on a physical iPhone speaker. Its physical gesture and
level are approved; the overall verdict remained `almost` because the melodic
variation while clicking around needs improvement. See `LISTENING-RESULT.md`.
