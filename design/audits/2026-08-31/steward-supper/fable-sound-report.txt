## What I authored

`steward-supper-music-v1` (same directory, one level up): a 28-bar piece at 60 bpm, written for the candlelit room and the Steward's supper by the rainy window. It uses the room's own modal voice with soft bloom attacks — the same acoustic physics as every approved tap, so taps land in tune by construction. Honestly stated: it is a lamplit, thumb-piano-adjacent tone, not a pretend piano; credible piano is beyond honest local synthesis and I didn't fake one. The rain isn't literal noise (that reads as hiss) — it's in the middle section's answers, which only ever step downward. The seam lands in the sparsest air; the drone is wrap-crossfaded and phrase tails wrap modulo the loop, so the repeat is a returning chapter rather than the fire-loop problem.
