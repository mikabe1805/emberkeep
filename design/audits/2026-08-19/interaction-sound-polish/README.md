# Interaction sound polish

## Semantic polish audition

The material contacts and approved hearth now have a broader product-level
follow-up under [`semantic-directions/`](semantic-directions/README.md). It
audits the overloaded legacy reward cues, defines the intended silence and
mixing rules, and provides three complete recorded-object/acoustic-instrument
directions plus a recommended intensity gradient. These are staging files only;
the runtime remains unchanged until a direction is approved.

## Intent

Keep the material-family idea, but let the room answer like a quiet fantasy
object rather than a collection of literal taps. Every voice begins with a
real physical contact, then blooms into a short acoustic interval from the
same D-centered suspended pentatonic palette. Reward sounds remain reserved
for a state change that has actually completed.

## Family language

| Family | Primary role | Contact character | Asset length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | ordinary controls | warm thumb-piano D/A beneath wood | 145-155 ms |
| Stone | room dock/navigation | mineral contact with a muted G/D resonance | 133-143 ms |
| Parchment | calendar and journal surfaces | paper gesture opening into a soft E/A breath | 160-170 ms |
| Brass | primary actions | wooden-mallet brass bowl in C/G | 180-190 ms |
| Glass | overlays and delicate dismissals | cloth-mallet glass bowl in A/E | 170-180 ms |

All contacts are mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM and acknowledge the touch inside the
first 8 ms. The musical bodies come from real CC0 acoustic recordings rather
than oscillators. The three variants in a family are baked and deterministic;
they retain the same interval so repeated taps do not accidentally compose a
melody.

## Listening files

- `interaction-sounds-audition.wav`: wood, stone, parchment, brass, then glass;
  three variants of each, with a longer pause between families.
- `interaction-sounds-before-after.wav`: one representative of each family in
  the same order from the cleaner-but-literal foley set, a longer pause, then
  the fantasy-musical set.
- `fire-ignite-before-after.wav`: the former brighter ignition, a long pause,
  then the new 1.6-second low fire swell. The user's YouTube example was used
  as an envelope and texture reference only; none of its audio is included.

Listen on a phone speaker at the app's normal device volume as the final taste
check. Headphones are useful for catching noise, but the phone speaker is the
actual interaction context.
