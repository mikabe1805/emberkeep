## Interaction direction

The current sound director and 110 room SFX are already identical in Build 41 and the later experiment. Keep the authored sound system; see sound-and-behavior.md for audited routes, cancellation and overlap rules.

- Accepted touch should produce a brief surface compression and one material contact. Drag-away and scrolling must remain scroll-safe; a down-event alone must not earn a success cue.
- A Quest completion responds locally first. Once the state mutation succeeds, its exact XP and domain delta settle into their permanent displays with one distinct completion voice. No second contact underneath it and no automatic next Quest.
- Goal progress changes only when the underlying accepted action earns it. The first Quest still waits for explicit Workshop acceptance; an already active Goal need not repeat that ceremony.
- Optional writing is owned by its source action and survives navigation and reload. Undo cannot erase later independent writing or stat activity.
- Returning retains existing earned work and useful prior context. Offer the existing next action and a concrete smaller-step adjustment; do not auto-award XP for returning, narrate missed days as debt, or force reflection.
- Reduce Motion must preserve causal order without camera travel. Sound mute must preserve all state feedback. Long text and assistive text sizes must be laid out by live widgets, never embedded into the art.

## Research and limits

The following are design inferences, not claims that a generated concept or RPG economy is proven to improve adherence. In Lally et al., consistent-context repetition was associated with increasing automaticity, with substantial variation; one missed opportunity did not materially change the formation process in that study. Keep a useful cue and avoid treating one lapse as lost progress. [Lally et al.](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ejsp.674)

A meta-analysis found different effects for controlling tangible/contingent rewards and positive feedback. Treat XP as clear evidence of a chosen action, keep agency and undo, and test its actual effects rather than asserting that points build habits. [Deci, Koestner and Ryan](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10589297/)

Both primary abstracts above were retrieved during this turn. Additional initiation, self-determination, self-compassion and gamification references, with calibrated implications, are in sound-and-behavior.md.
