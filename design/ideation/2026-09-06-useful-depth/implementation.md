## Implemented interaction contracts in this local slice

- Goals foregrounds the date-scoped one-to-three choice and keeps longer goals
  immediately below it. Reviewing a goal, creating one, and its Workshop remain
  separate actions. The first planned Quest still needs explicit acceptance.
- The shared chooser shows selected work, explainable suggestions and all other
  available ordinary Quests. Real timer filters describe one session, not the
  total length of the day. Selection never awards XP. A full field never evicts
  a choice. Cancel writes nothing; save preserves explicit order, including
  completed or set-aside choices the person kept.
- Timer Quests open an inert Ready workspace with their saved duration, current
  linked-plan guidance and latest real goal note when present. Start owns the
  deadline. Music is optional for this session. Honor and timed proof keep their
  existing distinct reward paths.
- A smaller or changed attempt stays a local draft until the review is accepted.
  Editing changes the draft only. Acceptance verifies the live identity before
  replacing the exact Quest and preserving its daily slot. Earlier notes and
  proof remain. Recovery changes an existing timer intentionally and restores
  the saved full practice after the recovery attempt.

## Shared material and response

The room, smoked working glass, cream EBGaramond display type and brass action
form one composition. Main Quests retains the earned room and its live RPG
progress; the writing-desk plate belongs to planning and session work.

Pressable supplies immediate local depression and keyboard/focus treatment.
Accepted selection, navigation and placement use the existing upgraded semantic
sound director. No countdown ticking or automatic next-Quest start was added.
Routes fade over 260 ms; reduced-motion access retains the same information.

The final working panes reuse the room's fine grain at low opacity. Primary
actions reuse the existing GoldSurface artwork and reflection; the chooser's
time choices share one adaptive rail. Large text wraps that rail without
removing choices. The timer uses a modal route with closed-loop keyboard focus,
and its clock remains readable to assistive technology without announcing every
second.

The first review render exposed an opaque material error: a border gradient
painted behind the translucent fill covered the entire pane. The corrected
surface paints only the rim, allowing the actual room and desktop texture
through a bounded blur. Rendered comparison is recorded in the root design QA.

## Research boundaries

The primary-source rationale and limits remain in README.md and the earlier
research-and-return.md. Suggestions are transparent local rules, not a learned
model. The owner's helpful experience with three choices is a product requirement,
not a claim that three is optimal for everyone.
