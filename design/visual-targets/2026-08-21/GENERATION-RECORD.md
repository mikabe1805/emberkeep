## Production prompt set

### Plans

Create a standalone 16:9 rainy glass conservatory interior in the existing Room
of Days palette. Use blackened iron mullions, ink-blue night, wet foliage, one
closed clothbound planning folio, restrained honey light, and a large quiet
lower-left field for live UI. No baked text, dates, people, or extra props.

The first production result was rejected because the cage light appeared behind
the wet pane while lighting the interior. The correction prompt preserved the
composition but required the fixture to sit on the camera-facing interior side
of the right mullion, with a visible mount, droplets behind it, and one coherent
light pool on the sill and folio.

## Selection rule

Option 3 is the active destination language. Option 1 remains the restraint
benchmark: each scene gets one meaningful place, one identifying artifact, and
only enough ornament to make its construction believable.
