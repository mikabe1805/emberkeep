## Product decisions after selection

The source is a direction, not a literal static screen. Owner feedback added two non-negotiable entry points already used in the product:

- `Unstick Me` stays directly discoverable and opens its existing focused launcher.
- `Guided Workouts` stays directly discoverable and hands off to its existing Quest-backed workout runner.

The production build therefore adds two scenic doors above the folio. Personal goals still follow the selected chapter structure:

1. user-authored reason;
2. real first proof or an honest legacy fallback;
3. one current action or a saved hard-day version;
4. earned milestone distance.

No chapter invents personal evidence. Legacy goals without stored proof show factual aggregate progress instead.

- Other goals exchange in the same folio with a 420 ms scale/fade/shared-axis settle.
