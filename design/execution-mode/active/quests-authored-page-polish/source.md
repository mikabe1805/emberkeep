## Direction and baseline

The existing Quests canon remains active: the earned room occupies the upper
first frame; XP and six domains share a darker optical-glass instrument panel;
one Main Quest may be featured; its category vignette sits low and integrated;
and `MARK COMPLETE` remains the single luminous action. The selected visual
target is the existing continuous moonlit-study direction, not a frozen palette
or a mandate to add ornament everywhere.

The pre-slice baseline is captured in
`design/comparisons/2026-09-26/quests-authored-page/current-430x932.png` and
`current-320x568-large.png`: the current Main Quest, HUD, and supporting board
are present, but the first-frame hierarchy can be made more intentional while
preserving live content and the established completion route. Those captures
are reference context for the forthcoming same-state comparison, not evidence
that the changed slice is acceptable.

## Bounded authority

This first slice may refine the visual hierarchy and material relationship
among the room/HUD, one active Main Quest, and the immediately supporting
board rail. It must preserve Quest data, completion semantics, existing
special-Quest flows, navigation, persistence, and the rule that the room stays
the world while content remains readable. A changed screen-wide composition,
new interaction model, or a visual departure from this canonical direction
requires a new direction choice rather than quiet expansion.
