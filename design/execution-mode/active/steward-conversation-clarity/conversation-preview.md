# Talk with the Steward

One complete conversation, in order. The replies marked **You** are the choices made in this example; you can choose other replies or leave at any time.

In the Workshop, select **Talk with the Steward** to begin. It does not create or change a Quest.

**Steward**

I was just reading a note from the cook downstairs. Apparently I complain too much about the soup.

**You:** What’s wrong with the soup?

**Steward**

Too much salt. I asked him to use less. Now he sends notes with my dinner.

**The cook’s note**

Less salt today.
Try it before you complain.

*He holds out the note.*

**Steward**

I’ve known him for years. He’s been teasing me about it all week.

**You:** I’m on his side.

**Steward**

You haven’t even tried the soup.

He’d like you.

**Steward**

His bread is good, though. He always saves me the end of the loaf. He knows that’s my favorite part.

**You:** You should tell him you like it.

**Steward**

I tell him. But I probably talk about the soup more.

**Steward**

Now I’m hungry. I hope he’s saved me some bread.

*He puts the note in the wooden box beside him.*

Select **Back to the workshop** when you’re ready. If you leave midway, **Continue talking** brings you back to your place.
